Method

Every score comes from public, unauthenticated requests. No account, no API key, no crawl beyond the homepage, one product page, and the standard policy endpoints.

  • AI crawler access — 20. A parsed robots.txt check for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, and Applebot-Extended.
  • llms.txt — 10. Whether a structured /llms.txt exists and leads with a top-level heading.
  • Entity markup — 15. Organization, WebSite, and navigation JSON-LD on the home page.
  • Product markup — 20. Product JSON-LD on one product page: name, price, currency, availability, image, and an identifier.
  • Catalog feed — 15. A public product feed plus a sitemap, plus a product sitemap where one exists.
  • Policies — 10. Shipping, returns, contact, and privacy links present in server-rendered markup.
  • Server rendering — 10. Visible character count in the raw HTML response.

Scores are a snapshot. A store can move twenty points in an afternoon with the right file and a template change.
